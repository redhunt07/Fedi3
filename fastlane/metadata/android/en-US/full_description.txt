Fedi3 is a self-hosted ActivityPub client. Your account, profile and posts live in a local Rust core on the device, while a relay you control carries federation traffic when the phone is offline.

Features:
- Your identity and data stay on your device; the relay stores only sealed chat envelopes and forwards ActivityPub traffic.
- Multi-device sync over P2P, with QR pairing to link a new phone or PC and transfer the account, profile and chat history.
- Chat with direct and group threads, voice and video notes, and calls with screen sharing.
- Push-only background notifications: a foreground service polls the relay spool and starts the core only when something arrives, then stops it.
- Full ActivityPub timelines, profiles, follow requests and moderation tools.
- Optional self-hosted push gateway (Web Push or ntfy) configured per relay.
- No proprietary tracking, no Google Play Services required.
